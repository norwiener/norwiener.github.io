Shinhan

(Click the image for a surprise!)

A mysterious, three-antennaed creature who stands as a legendary guardian

John Tien Shinhan is a surprisingly emotional warrior. He may burst into tears of pure anxiety after a difficult battle, only to unleash a devastating energy attack moments later. His unusual combination of cuteness, courage, and overwhelming strength has earned him a place among the most enigmatic figures in the kingdom.

Personality: A disciplined, stoic, and intensely focused on becoming stronger. Beneath his calm exterior, however, lies a deeply anxious creature who feels the emotional weight of every battle.

Fun Fact: Shinhan's relationship with Rakko is one of mutual respect and martial discipline. Rakko's strength and reputation as an elite monster hunter serve as an inspiration for Shinhan's relentless pursuit of improvement.
