The legendary monster hunter

Rakko is a powerful and highly respected hunter who takes on dangerous monster-subjugation jobs. They are admired by Chiikawa and Hachiware and are willing to help train them to become stronger.

Personality: Brave, disciplined, generous, and dependable.

Fun fact: Despite their formidable reputation and sword skills, Rakko has a serious sweet tooth.
