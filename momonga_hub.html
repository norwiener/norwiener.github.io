The attention-seeking troublemaker

Momonga is a flying-squirrel-like character who loves being told how cute they are. They deliberately act adorable to get attention, food, and favors, and can be demanding, selfish, and mischievous.

Personality: Self-centered, dramatic, cunning, and attention-seeking.

Fun fact: Momonga's story has a major mystery involving Dekatsuyo and a body swap, making this character more complicated than their cute appearance suggests.
