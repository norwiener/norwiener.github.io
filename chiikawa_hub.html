Chiikawa

The shy, sweet protagonist

Chiikawa is a tiny, gentle creature who is easily frightened and often cries when things become stressful. They can be timid and lack confidence, but they try hard, care deeply about their friends, and face challenges even when they're scared.

Personality: Shy, sensitive, kind, and determined.

Fun fact: Chiikawa works to earn money and obtain rewards by taking on jobs and monster-subjugation tasks with friends.
