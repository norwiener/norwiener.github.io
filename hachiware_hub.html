The optimistic best friend

Hachiware is a cheerful, talkative, and resourceful friend who usually looks for the bright side of a difficult situation. They support Chiikawa when things get scary and often help keep the group together.

Personality: Positive, thoughtful, brave, and sociable.

Fun fact: Despite the cat-like ears and appearance, Hachiware isn't necessarily a cat. Hachiware is also known for their love of singing and playing music.
