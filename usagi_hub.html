The chaotic, fearless friend

Usagi is energetic, unpredictable, and almost always ready for an adventure. They often communicate through loud shouts such as “Yaha!” and “Ura!” rather than ordinary conversation. Although mischievous, Usagi can be surprisingly clever and capable when danger strikes.

Personality: Fearless, playful, spontaneous, and clever.

Fun fact: Usagi's home remains a mystery, and their exact species is not definitively established despite their rabbit-like appearance.
